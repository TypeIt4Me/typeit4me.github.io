How It Works

Step 1: Define Snippets

Open TypeIt4Me, create sets, and add abbreviations linked to your full text, formatted content, or images.

Step 2: Type Anywhere

Switch to any app. Enter your abbreviation followed by a trigger (like space or punctuation).

Step 3: Instant Expansion

TypeIt4Me replaces it on the fly with your snippet. Edit, reorder, or prioritize as needed—no restarts required.

Step 4: Refine & Automate

Use prompts, variables, or options for dynamic snippets. Menu access keeps everything at your fingertips.
